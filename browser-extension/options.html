XGUARD / BUYER SIDE

Connect XGuard once.

The extension only sends a payment intent after you choose Use XGuard. Showing the offer or choosing “Continue without XGuard” does not call the paid decision endpoint.

The key is stored in Chrome extension local storage and is sent only to xguardgate.com.

Do not enter card numbers, CVV/CVC, PINs, private keys, seed phrases or wallet mnemonics into XGuard. The Payment Decision API rejects credential-shaped fields.
