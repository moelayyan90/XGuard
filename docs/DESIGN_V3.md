# XGuard Portal V3

Design intent: institutional execution infrastructure, not a generic SaaS landing page.

- light paper background, black typography, one blue signal color
- no gradients, glows, rounded feature cards, animated timelines, or decorative dashboards
- editorial grid and policy tables instead of marketing tiles
- execution contract and bounded 10–50ms policy are the primary visual content
- no fabricated BAM production traffic or performance claims
