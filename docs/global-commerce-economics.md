# Unit economics

`revenue = target buyer unit price × buyer quantity`

`landed cost = supplier unit cost × quantity + shipping + customs + tax + payment fees + insurance + other modeled cost`

`reserve = max(USD 25, 5% of landed cost)`

`net profit = revenue - landed cost - reserve`

`margin bps = net profit / revenue × 10,000`

Mainnet defaults require at least USD 100 modeled net profit and 2,000 bps modeled margin before an opportunity can be READY.
